---
title: "Server Rules"
description: "General rules and guidelines for the VanillaPlus community."
section: "General"
order: 1
---

1. **No Exploiting** — Do not abuse bugs, glitches, or unintended mechanics. If you find one, report it through the bug tracker. Exploiting for personal gain will result in immediate action.

2. **No Harassment** — Treat all players with respect. Targeted harassment, stalking, or sustained toxic behavior towards any player will not be tolerated.

3. **No Botting or Automation** — The use of third-party software to automate gameplay is strictly prohibited. This includes bots, auto-clickers, rotation helpers, and any software that plays the game for you.

4. **No Account Trading or Selling** — Your account is yours. Do not buy, sell, trade, or give away accounts. Account sharing is discouraged and done at your own risk — we will not restore items lost to shared access.

5. **Respect Game Masters** — GMs are here to help. Follow their instructions and do not impersonate, threaten, or argue abusively with staff members. If you disagree with a decision, use the proper appeal channels.

6. **No Racism, Sexism, or Hate Speech** — Zero tolerance. Slurs, hate symbols, and discriminatory language of any kind will result in an immediate and permanent ban.

7. **No Spamming** — Do not flood chat channels, mailbox, or any in-game communication system. This includes gold seller advertisements, repeated messages, and ASCII art spam.

8. **English in World Channels** — Please use English in global and world chat channels. You are welcome to use any language in private channels, guild chat, and party chat.

9. **No Multiboxing in PvP** — Multiboxing is allowed for PvE content only. Using multiple accounts simultaneously in any PvP scenario — battlegrounds, world PvP, or duels — is prohibited.

10. **Have Fun** — At the end of the day, we're all here to enjoy the game. Help new players, contribute to the community, and make VanillaPlus a place people want to call home.
