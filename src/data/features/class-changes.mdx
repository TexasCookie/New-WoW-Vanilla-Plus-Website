## Philosophy

Vanilla WoW had brilliant class design at its core, but the reality was that many specs were simply non-viable. Our philosophy is **spec viability without homogenization** — every talent tree should offer a meaningful playstyle without making classes feel identical. We've kept the iconic feel of each class while tuning numbers, adding key abilities, and adjusting talent positions to create real build diversity.

## What Changed

Each class received targeted adjustments based on thousands of hours of PTR testing and community feedback. Changes range from simple coefficient tweaks to new baseline abilities. We focused on three areas: **solo viability** (every spec should be able to quest comfortably), **group value** (every spec should bring something unique to a dungeon or raid), and **PvP balance** (no spec should be a free kill).

## Classes

### Druid
Bear and Cat form scaling has been improved across the board. Balance now receives Innervate baseline, and Restoration's talent tree has been reorganized to reduce mandatory filler talents. Feral tanks can now compete with warriors for main tank slots.

### Hunter
Pet AI has been improved with better pathing and stance management. Survival has been reworked into a viable melee/ranged hybrid tree. Traps can now be placed in combat with a reduced cooldown. Several pet families have received new unique abilities.

### Mage
Arcane has been given a proper rotation with Arcane Blast now available as a talented spell. Fire's mana efficiency has been improved. Frost receives a new talent that makes shatter combos more accessible. All three specs are now competitive raid choices.

### Paladin
Protection has been reworked into a full tanking spec with a proper taunt and improved threat generation. Retribution now scales correctly with attack power and spell damage. Holy remains the premier tank healer with improved Beacon of Light functionality.

### Priest
Shadow now offers proper raid DPS with Vampiric Touch available earlier in the tree. Discipline has been expanded as a damage-mitigation healer with Power Word: Barrier as a new talented ability. Holy's healing output has been tuned to match its Mana constraints.

### Rogue
All three specs now offer distinct combat patterns. Subtlety received improved openers and a new combo point generator. Assassination poison mechanics have been streamlined. Combat now scales better with weapon specializations across all weapon types.

### Shaman
Enhancement has been given proper itemization support and a new strike ability. Elemental's cast time issues have been addressed with improved pushback resistance. Restoration now competes directly with priest healing throughput. Two-handed weapon talents have been moved deeper into Enhancement.

### Warlock
Demonology now allows the Warlock to sacrifice their demon for a meaningful buff without losing all pet utility. Affliction's DoT scaling has been improved so it stays relevant in longer fights. Destruction's burst rotation has been refined with better Soul Shard economy.

### Warrior
Arms is now a competitive PvE DPS tree alongside Fury. Protection received quality-of-life improvements including reduced shield block cooldown and better rage generation when not being hit. Fury's hit cap issues have been addressed with a new talent that converts excess hit rating to critical strike rating.
