Welcome to **VanillaPlus** — a private World of Warcraft server that takes everything you love about Classic WoW and pushes it further. We're not a blizzlike clone. We're a love letter to Vanilla, written by people who have been playing it for nearly two decades.

Our goal is simple: preserve the soul of the original game while introducing rebalanced classes, new raid bosses, and quality-of-life improvements that respect the Vanilla philosophy. Every class has been carefully tuned so that every spec is viable in both PvE and PvP. No more being benched because you chose the "wrong" talent tree. Whether you want to tank as a Paladin, DPS as a Balance Druid, or heal as a Shadow Priest — you can do it here.

What truly sets VanillaPlus apart is our community-driven approach. Major changes are discussed openly, tested on PTR with real player feedback, and only shipped when the community is satisfied. Bug reports are handled transparently through our public GitHub tracker. Our development team is made up of longtime private server veterans who understand that the players are the heart of any server. Join us and help shape the future of Classic WoW.
