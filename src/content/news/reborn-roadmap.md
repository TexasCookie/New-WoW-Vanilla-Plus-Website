The new **VanillaPlus Reborn** portal is designed as a long-term home for the project instead of a small landing page.

This first stage includes:

- a high-impact home page with strong entry points for play, account creation, donations, and Discord
- a server-rendered Astro 6 foundation ready for future private pages and account tools
- React islands for status widgets, login/register flows, and donation UI
- a content collection for news so announcements can scale without turning into hard-coded page sections

The next phase is expected to connect real account, payments, and live server data.
