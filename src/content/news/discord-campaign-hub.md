Discord is no longer treated as an afterthought.

The site now gives Discord its own entry page so the team can later expand it into:

- support and launcher troubleshooting channels
- event and battleground signups
- role sync for supporters or testers
- Discord OAuth account linking

This makes the website feel like a connected game platform rather than a collection of separate utility links.
